# Raw Dataset Sources

## PhishTank

Source:
PhishTank Developer Data Feed

Purpose:
Current verified phishing URLs.

Original format:
CSV

Project role:
Supplementary phishing scam data.

Official source:
http://data.phishtank.com/data/online-valid.csv

Notes:
The PhishTank downloadable database contains verified online phishing URLs.

Source:
ScamFerret research artifact

Authors:
Hiroki Nakano, Takashi Koide and Daiki Chiba

Project:
ScamFerret: Detecting Scam Websites Autonomously with Large Language Models

Official repository:
https://github.com/ScamFerret/artifact

Project role:
Principal multi-category scam and legitimate website source.

Categories used:
- cryptocurrency
- investment
- online shopping (English)
- technical support

Notes:
The raw URL files must remain unchanged.
